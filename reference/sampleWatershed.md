# Sample watershed

A dataset containing a time series of daily rainfall and measured water
runoff in a gauged watershed over 39 years

## Usage

``` r
sampleWatershed
```

## Format

A data frame with 14245 rows and 3 columns:

- date:

  date variable (daily measurements) between October 01, 1979 and
  September 30, 2018

- rain:

  daily cumulated rainfall

- gauge:

  daily cumulated, gauged watershed runoff

## Source

processed by HydroGeoScience for Watershed Management Research group at
the University of British Columbia, Vancouver, Canada
